How it works

Creator fees go to a burn wallet. A bot uses them to buy $CHONKS and $STONK and burn both, half and half. Every burn is a Solana transaction that anyone can check.

Where the fees go

50%50%

  • 50% burns $CHONKS. Fees buy it back and burn it.

  • 50% burns $STONK. Fees buy it and burn it too.

Anything that arrives as $CHONKS or $STONK is split the same way, so every burn ends up 50/50.

How a burn happens

  • Fees land. They collect in the burn wallet.

  • About $20 builds up. The bot buys $CHONKS and $STONK, half and half.

  • Everything is burned. It then waits at least 5 minutes before the next burn.

The bot checks the price impact first and skips a swap when the market is too thin, so some burns wait.

Burns depend on creator fees and market conditions. Nothing here is guaranteed.
